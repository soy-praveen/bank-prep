# Puzzles & Seating — Practice

Five self-contained SBI PO Prelims puzzle/seating sets (25 questions). Each set has a single unique arrangement; full clues are inline. Work the setup first, then check the Answer Key for the solved grid, per-question reasons, and the solving trick.

---

## Questions

### Set 1 — Linear seating (single row, single direction)

Seven people — A, B, C, D, E, F and G — sit in a straight row. All of them face north. Positions are counted from left to right, so position 1 is the leftmost seat and position 7 is the rightmost. In every clue, "left" means toward position 1 and "right" means toward position 7.

Clues:
1. C sits exactly in the middle of the row.
2. D sits at the extreme left end.
3. There are three persons sitting between D and F.
4. A sits immediately to the left of C.
5. G sits at the extreme right end.
6. B sits immediately to the right of D.

**Q1.** Who sits at the extreme right end?
a) E   b) F   c) G   d) D   e) A

**Q2.** How many persons sit between B and F?
a) 1   b) 2   c) 3   d) 4   e) None

**Q3.** Who sits third to the right of A?
a) C   b) F   c) E   d) G   e) B

**Q4.** Which of the following pairs are the two immediate neighbours of C?
a) B and F   b) A and F   c) A and E   d) F and E   e) D and A

**Q5.** Who sits exactly between D and A?
a) C   b) F   c) E   d) B   e) A

### Set 2 — Circular seating (eight people, facing the centre)

Eight people — P, Q, R, S, T, U, V and W — sit around a circular table and all of them face the centre. Because they face the centre, each person's left hand points in the clockwise direction and each person's right hand points in the anticlockwise direction. So "to the left" means the next seat clockwise and "to the right" means the next seat anticlockwise.

Clues:
1. T sits immediately to the left of P.
2. R sits second to the left of P.
3. Q sits exactly opposite P.
4. W sits immediately to the right of Q.
5. U sits immediately to the left of Q.
6. S sits second to the right of P.
7. V sits immediately to the right of P.

**Q1.** Who sits exactly opposite P?
a) U   b) Q   c) S   d) W   e) T

**Q2.** Who sits third to the left of U?
a) P   b) V   c) S   d) R   e) W

**Q3.** How many people sit between T and Q when counted clockwise from T?
a) 1   b) 2   c) 3   d) 4   e) None

**Q4.** Who sits second to the right of W?
a) R   b) P   c) T   d) S   e) U

**Q5.** Which pair sits immediately adjacent to S (on either side)?
a) U and V   b) Q and U   c) P and V   d) T and R   e) W and Q

### Set 3 — Floors + car colour (double variable)

A seven-storey building has floors numbered 1 (lowest) to 7 (topmost). Seven people — L, M, N, O, P, Q and R — live on these floors, one person per floor. Each owns a car of a different colour among Red, Blue, Green, White, Black, Yellow and Pink.

Clues:
1. Only two people live above P.
2. M lives on an even-numbered floor that is above P.
3. R lives immediately above M.
4. Q lives on the lowest floor.
5. N lives immediately above Q.
6. L lives immediately below O.
7. The person on the topmost floor owns the Pink car.
8. Q owns the Red car.
9. N owns the Blue car.
10. The Black car owner lives immediately below the Pink car owner.
11. The Green car owner lives immediately above the White car owner.
12. The Yellow car owner lives immediately above the Green car owner.

**Q1.** Who lives on floor 4?
a) P   b) O   c) L   d) M   e) N

**Q2.** Which colour car does the person on floor 3 own?
a) Green   b) Blue   c) White   d) Black   e) Yellow

**Q3.** How many people live between the Blue car owner and the Black car owner?
a) 2   b) 3   c) 4   d) 1   e) 5

**Q4.** Who owns the Yellow car?
a) O   b) M   c) P   d) R   e) L

**Q5.** M owns which colour car?
a) Pink   b) Black   c) Green   d) Yellow   e) Blue

### Set 4 — Months + city (double variable)

Six people — P, Q, R, S, T and U — each attend a seminar in a different month. The six months, in chronological order, are January, March, May, July, September and November. Each person is from a different city among Delhi, Mumbai, Pune, Kochi, Patna and Surat.

Clues:
1. R attends in November (the last of the six months).
2. Only one person attends before S.
3. P attends immediately before S.
4. T attends immediately before R.
5. Q attends in an earlier month than U.
6. The person attending in January is from Delhi.
7. R is from Patna.
8. The Mumbai person attends immediately after the Delhi person.
9. Q is from Surat.
10. The Pune person attends immediately before the Kochi person.

**Q1.** Who attends in July?
a) Q   b) T   c) U   d) P   e) S

**Q2.** Q is from which city?
a) Pune   b) Surat   c) Delhi   d) Kochi   e) Patna

**Q3.** How many people attend between the Delhi person and the Kochi person?
a) 2   b) 3   c) 4   d) 1   e) 5

**Q4.** Who attends immediately before R?
a) U   b) Q   c) T   d) S   e) P

**Q5.** The person from Pune attends in which month?
a) May   b) July   c) September   d) March   e) January

### Set 5 — Box stacking (seven boxes, one above another)

Seven boxes — A, B, C, D, E, F and G — are stacked one directly above another. Position 1 is the bottom of the stack and position 7 is the top.

Clues:
1. Only two boxes are kept above D.
2. G is kept immediately below D.
3. F is kept at the bottom of the stack.
4. C is kept immediately above F.
5. A is kept between C and G.
6. E is kept at the top of the stack.

**Q1.** Which box is at the top of the stack?
a) B   b) D   c) E   d) G   e) A

**Q2.** How many boxes are kept between F and D?
a) 2   b) 3   c) 4   d) 1   e) 5

**Q3.** Which box is kept immediately above A?
a) D   b) G   c) C   d) B   e) F

**Q4.** Which box is kept just below B?
a) G   b) D   c) E   d) A   e) C

**Q5.** Counting from the bottom, which box is fourth?
a) A   b) C   c) G   d) D   e) B

## Answer Key, Solutions & Tricks

### Set 1 — Linear seating

Solved arrangement (left to right):

| Pos 1 | Pos 2 | Pos 3 | Pos 4 | Pos 5 | Pos 6 | Pos 7 |
|-------|-------|-------|-------|-------|-------|-------|
| D | B | A | C | F | E | G |

How it locks: C is the middle of 7 seats, so C = 4 (clue 1). D is the extreme left, D = 1 (clue 2). Three persons between D and F places F at seat 5 (clue 3). A immediately left of C gives A = 3 (clue 4). G at the extreme right, G = 7 (clue 5). B immediately right of D gives B = 2 (clue 6). Only seat 6 is left, so E = 6.

- **Q1 → c) G.** G is fixed at the extreme right end (seat 7).
- **Q2 → b) 2.** Between B (2) and F (5) sit A and C — two persons.
- **Q3 → c) E.** Third to the right of A (3) is seat 6, which is E.
- **Q4 → b) A and F.** C is at seat 4; its neighbours are A (3) and F (5).
- **Q5 → d) B.** Between D (1) and A (3) is seat 2, which is B.

**Trick:** Anchor the fixed "end" and "middle" clues first (D, C, G), then hang the "immediately/…between" clues off them. "N persons between X and Y" leaves N seats in the gap, so three between D(1) puts F at 1+3+1 = 5. Fill the last empty seat by elimination rather than searching for a clue for it.

### Set 2 — Circular seating (facing centre)

Solved arrangement, read clockwise starting at P:

| Clockwise order | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 | 7 | 8 |
|-----------------|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Person | P | T | R | W | Q | U | S | V |

How it locks (facing centre → left = clockwise, right = anticlockwise). Fix P. T immediately left (clockwise) of P (clue 1). R second left of P, i.e. next clockwise after T (clue 2). Q opposite P, four seats away (clue 3). W immediately right (anticlockwise) of Q sits just before Q clockwise (clue 4); U immediately left of Q sits just after Q clockwise (clue 5). S second right of P and V immediately right of P fill the two seats on P's anticlockwise side (clues 6, 7). The clockwise ring is P, T, R, W, Q, U, S, V.

- **Q1 → b) Q.** Q is defined as directly opposite P.
- **Q2 → b) V.** Third to the left (clockwise) of U: U → S → V → … lands on V.
- **Q3 → b) 2.** Clockwise from T to Q you pass R and W — two people.
- **Q4 → c) T.** Second to the right (anticlockwise) of W: W → R → T lands on T.
- **Q5 → a) U and V.** S sits between U (clockwise side) and V (anticlockwise side).

**Trick:** Convert the direction words once — facing centre, left is clockwise and right is anticlockwise — then treat the ring like a clock. "Nth to the left" means N steps clockwise (N−1 people sit in between). Draw one circle, pin P, and place everything relative to P so rotational symmetry never confuses you.

### Set 3 — Floors + car colour

Solved arrangement (top to bottom):

| Floor | Person | Car colour |
|-------|--------|-----------|
| 7 | R | Pink |
| 6 | M | Black |
| 5 | P | Yellow |
| 4 | O | Green |
| 3 | L | White |
| 2 | N | Blue |
| 1 | Q | Red |

Pass 1 (positions): "Only two above P" → P = 5 (clue 1). The even floor above 5 is 6, so M = 6 (clue 2), and R immediately above M → R = 7 (clue 3). Q lowest → floor 1, N immediately above → floor 2 (clues 4, 5). That leaves floors 3 and 4 for L and O with L immediately below O (clue 6): L = 3, O = 4.

Pass 2 (colours via bridge clues): topmost owns Pink → R = Pink (clue 7). Q = Red (clue 8), N = Blue (clue 9). Black immediately below Pink → floor 6 = M = Black (clue 10). The Yellow-Green-White stack must be consecutive with Yellow on top (clues 11, 12); the only free consecutive floors are 5-4-3, giving P = Yellow, O = Green, L = White.

- **Q1 → b) O.** Floor 4 is O.
- **Q2 → c) White.** Floor 3 is L, who owns White.
- **Q3 → b) 3.** Blue is floor 2, Black is floor 6; floors 3, 4, 5 lie between — three people.
- **Q4 → c) P.** Yellow is floor 5, which is P.
- **Q5 → b) Black.** M is on floor 6, the Black car owner.

**Trick:** Two-pass grid. Solve all the floor positions first using only the position clues (P, M, R, Q, N, then L/O by elimination). Only then bring in the colour clues, which act as bridges: a fixed anchor (Pink on top, Red for Q) plus a rigid consecutive block (Yellow-Green-White) drops straight into the one gap left.

### Set 4 — Months + city

Solved arrangement (earliest to latest):

| Month | Person | City |
|-------|--------|------|
| January | P | Delhi |
| March | S | Mumbai |
| May | Q | Surat |
| July | U | Pune |
| September | T | Kochi |
| November | R | Patna |

Pass 1 (months): R = November (clue 1). "Only one person before S" → S is 2nd = March (clue 2); P immediately before S → P = January (clue 3). T immediately before R → T = September (clue 4). The two open months, May and July, go to Q and U with Q earlier (clue 5): Q = May, U = July.

Pass 2 (cities via bridge clues): January person is Delhi → P = Delhi (clue 6). R = Patna (clue 7). Mumbai immediately after Delhi → March = S = Mumbai (clue 8). Q = Surat (clue 9). The remaining Pune and Kochi are consecutive with Pune first (clue 10); the only free consecutive slot is July-September, so U = Pune, T = Kochi.

- **Q1 → c) U.** July is U.
- **Q2 → b) Surat.** Q is stated to be from Surat.
- **Q3 → b) 3.** Delhi = January, Kochi = September; March, May, July lie between — three people.
- **Q4 → c) T.** T (September) attends immediately before R (November).
- **Q5 → b) July.** Pune is U, who attends in July.

**Trick:** Treat the six ordered months exactly like six seats in a row and solve positions first (anchor R at the end, count "only one before S," chain the immediate-before clues). Then layer cities on with the same bridge idea: one anchor (Delhi in January) plus a consecutive Pune-Kochi block fills the last gap.

### Set 5 — Box stacking

Solved arrangement (top to bottom):

| Position | Box |
|----------|-----|
| 7 (top) | E |
| 6 | B |
| 5 | D |
| 4 | G |
| 3 | A |
| 2 | C |
| 1 (bottom) | F |

How it locks: "Only two boxes above D" → D = 5 (clue 1). G immediately below D → G = 4 (clue 2). F at the bottom → F = 1 (clue 3); C immediately above F → C = 2 (clue 4). A between C (2) and G (4) → A = 3 (clue 5). E at the top → E = 7 (clue 6). The lone empty slot 6 takes B.

- **Q1 → c) E.** E is fixed at the top.
- **Q2 → b) 3.** Between F (1) and D (5) sit C, A and G — three boxes.
- **Q3 → b) G.** Immediately above A (3) is position 4, box G.
- **Q4 → b) D.** Just below B (6) is position 5, box D.
- **Q5 → c) G.** Fourth from the bottom is position 4, box G.

**Trick:** Vertical stacks are just a linear row turned upright — fix the "top / bottom / N above" anchors first (D from "two above," F and E from the ends), then place the "immediately above/below" and "between" boxes off them. Whenever every clue is used but one box is unplaced, drop it into the single empty position by elimination instead of hunting for another clue.
