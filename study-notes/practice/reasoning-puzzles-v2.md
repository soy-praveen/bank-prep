# Puzzles & Seating — Practice (Vol. 2)

SBI PO Prelims level (moderate). Six sets, thirty questions. Every set has exactly one valid arrangement. Attempt each fully before checking the key. Standard circular convention used: **a person facing the centre has their immediate left in the clockwise direction and immediate right in the anticlockwise direction.**

---

## Questions

### Set 1 — Linear row (seven people, all facing north)

Seven friends — Karan, Lata, Manoj, Nisha, Omar, Priya and Rahul — sit in a straight row facing north. Positions are counted 1 (leftmost) to 7 (rightmost).

Clues:
1. Manoj sits exactly in the middle of the row.
2. Priya sits immediately to the left of Manoj.
3. Rahul sits at one of the extreme ends, and Omar sits immediately to the left of Rahul.
4. Nisha sits at an extreme end.
5. Exactly one person sits between Priya and Lata.
6. Karan does not sit at either extreme end.

**Q1.** Who sits third from the left end?
a) Manoj  b) Priya  c) Karan  d) Lata  e) Omar

**Q2.** How many persons sit between Karan and Omar?
a) two  b) three  c) four  d) one  e) five

**Q3.** Who sits immediately to the right of Lata?
a) Manoj  b) Rahul  c) Omar  d) Priya  e) No one

**Q4.** Which pair sits at the two extreme ends?
a) Nisha and Omar  b) Karan and Rahul  c) Nisha and Rahul  d) Priya and Rahul  e) Nisha and Manoj

**Q5.** Who sits second to the left of Omar?
a) Priya  b) Lata  c) Manoj  d) Karan  e) Nisha

## Answer Key, Solutions & Tricks

### Set 1 — solved arrangement

Left (1) to right (7), all facing north:

| Pos 1 | Pos 2 | Pos 3 | Pos 4 | Pos 5 | Pos 6 | Pos 7 |
|-------|-------|-------|-------|-------|-------|-------|
| Nisha | Karan | Priya | Manoj | Lata  | Omar  | Rahul |

Deduction: Manoj is centre (pos 4); Priya immediately left → pos 3. Omar-then-Rahul must sit at the right end (Rahul needs a seat to his left), so Omar = 6, Rahul = 7. Nisha then takes the only free end, pos 1. One person between Priya (3) and Lata → Lata = 5 (pos 1 is taken). Karan fills pos 2, satisfying "not at an end."

- **Q1 → b) Priya**
- **Q2 → b) three** (positions 3, 4, 5 lie between Karan at 2 and Omar at 6)
- **Q3 → c) Omar**
- **Q4 → c) Nisha and Rahul**
- **Q5 → c) Manoj**

**Trick:** In "north-facing" rows, left/right match the reader's own left/right, so pin the fixed anchor (the middle person) first, then attach the forced block (Omar–Rahul) to whichever end still allows a neighbour. Ends fill last.

### Set 2 — solved arrangement

Clockwise order around the table (all facing centre):

| Seat | Person |
|------|--------|
| 1 | Amit |
| 2 | Bina |
| 3 | Farhan |
| 4 | Chetan |
| 5 | Esha |
| 6 | Harsh |
| 7 | Gita |
| 8 | Divya |

Deduction (left = clockwise, right = anticlockwise): fix Amit; Divya is his right neighbour. Chetan third to Amit's left, Bina second to Chetan's right, Farhan on Bina's left, Esha on Chetan's left, Gita second to Esha's left, Harsh on Gita's right — each clue places one person, closing the circle uniquely.

- **Q6 → c) Divya** (two seats to Harsh's left: Gita then Divya)
- **Q7 → a) Farhan**
- **Q8 → b) Amit** (Esha and Amit are 4 seats apart, i.e. directly opposite)
- **Q9 → b) Harsh and Divya**
- **Q10 → c) Gita** (three seats to Bina's right: Amit, Divya, Gita)

**Trick:** Write "facing centre → left is clockwise" at the top of your rough diagram. Then every "left" step moves clockwise and every "right" step anticlockwise, so you never flip direction mid-solve. "Second to the right" = skip one seat anticlockwise.

### Set 3 — solved arrangement

| Floor | Person | Colour |
|-------|--------|--------|
| 7 (top) | Uma | Black |
| 6 | Ravi | Yellow |
| 5 | Wasim | Green |
| 4 | Sonia | Red |
| 3 | Yash | White |
| 2 | Vikas | Pink |
| 1 (bottom) | Tarun | Blue |

Deduction: Sonia = 4, Uma = 7, Ravi = 6. "Two between Sonia and Tarun, Tarun below" → Tarun = 1. Yash immediately above Vikas: the only consecutive pair left in {2,3,5} is (2,3), so Vikas = 2, Yash = 3, leaving Wasim = 5. Colours: Green immediately above Red (floor 4) → Wasim (5) Green; Yellow immediately below Uma → Ravi (6) Yellow; the last colour, Pink, goes to Vikas.

- **Q11 → b) Wasim**
- **Q12 → b) Black**
- **Q13 → c) three** (floors 3, 4, 5 lie between Vikas at 2 and Ravi at 6)
- **Q14 → c) Vikas**
- **Q15 → b) Wasim – floor 5 – Green**

**Trick:** Two-variable floor puzzles: lock every floor first using position clues, then overlay the attribute using "immediately above/below" links that hinge on an already-fixed floor (here Red on floor 4 fixes Green on floor 5). Assign the leftover attribute last.

### Set 4 — solved arrangement

Earliest to latest:

| Order | Month | Person |
|-------|-------|--------|
| 1 | January | Rekha |
| 2 | February | Naina |
| 3 | April | Sahil |
| 4 | July | Puja |
| 5 | September | Qadir |
| 6 | November | Ovais |

Deduction: Puja = July (4). One person between Puja and Naina with Naina earlier → Naina = 2. Ovais latest → November (6); Qadir immediately before → September (5). Sahil immediately before Puja → April (3). Rekha, the earliest, takes January.

- **Q16 → b) Sahil**
- **Q17 → c) two** (orders 3 and 4 lie between Naina at 2 and Qadir at 5)
- **Q18 → c) Qadir**
- **Q19 → b) January**
- **Q20 → c) Sahil**

**Trick:** Convert months to a 1–6 timeline before reading clues; "travels before/after" then becomes simply "smaller/larger number," and "earliest/latest" nails down the two ends instantly.

### Set 5 — solved arrangement

| Position | Box |
|----------|-----|
| 7 (top) | Q |
| 6 | P |
| 5 | T |
| 4 | W |
| 3 | R |
| 2 | U |
| 1 (bottom) | S |

Deduction: "Only two boxes above T" → T = 5; W immediately below → W = 4. "Two boxes between W and S, S below" → S = 1. U immediately above S → 2; R immediately above U → 3. Q at top → 7, leaving P = 6.

- **Q21 → c) S**
- **Q22 → c) two** (positions 4 and 5 lie between R at 3 and P at 6)
- **Q23 → b) T**
- **Q24 → c) W** (position 4 of 7)
- **Q25 → a) Q is kept immediately above P**

**Trick:** "Only two boxes above X" fixes X directly (7 − 2 = position 5); count-from-the-named-end phrasing is the fastest anchor in stack puzzles. Build the rest as a chain of "immediately above/below" links.

### Set 6 — solved arrangement

| Day | Person |
|-----|--------|
| Monday | Girish |
| Tuesday | Aarti |
| Wednesday | Dev |
| Thursday | Bunty |
| Friday | Hema |
| Saturday | Cyrus |
| Sunday | Farah |

Deduction: Bunty = Thursday (4). Two people between Bunty and Girish, Girish earlier → Girish = Monday (1). Aarti immediately after Girish → Tuesday (2); Dev immediately after Aarti → Wednesday (3). Farah = Sunday (7); Cyrus immediately before → Saturday (6). Hema immediately after Bunty → Friday (5).

- **Q26 → b) Dev**
- **Q27 → c) three** (Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday lie between Girish on Monday and Hema on Friday)
- **Q28 → c) Hema**
- **Q29 → b) Tuesday**
- **Q30 → c) four** (Thursday, Friday, Saturday, Sunday attendees come after Dev on Wednesday)

**Trick:** Map Monday–Sunday to 1–7 and anchor the fixed day (Thursday) plus the "N-between" clue first — that clue usually has only one legal placement once you know the direction ("Girish attends before"). Then the immediate-after chain fills the rest in one sweep.
