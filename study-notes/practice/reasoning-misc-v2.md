## Answer Key, Solutions & Tricks

### Blood Relations

**Q1. (a) Brother.** "Only daughter of my grandfather" = Meera's father's only sister OR Meera's mother. Since the grandfather has an only daughter, and Meera exists, that only daughter is Meera's mother. The man is the son of Meera's mother, i.e. Meera's brother.
**Trick:** Convert possessive chains inward-out. "Only daughter of grandfather" who is a parent of the speaker must be the speaker's mother; her son is your brother.

**Q2. (c) Father-in-law.** "Grandson of my mother" = Rahul's son (mother's grandson through Rahul). The girl is the wife of Rahul's son, so Rahul is her father-in-law.
**Trick:** Mother's grandson = your son (assuming male line through the speaker). Wife of your son => you are father-in-law.

**Q3. (c) Nephew.** A is father of B; E is A's brother, so E is B's uncle. D is B's wife; C is D's son, so C is B's son. C is the son of E's nephew (B), making C the grand-nephew... check: B is E's nephew (brother's son). C is B's son = E's grand-nephew. Re-evaluate: E's brother is A; A's son is B => B is E's nephew. C is B's son => C is E's grand-nephew.
**Corrected answer: (b) Grand-nephew.**
**Trick:** Draw the tree by generation. E and A are one generation; B is next; C is the third. E to C spans two generations down a sibling line => grand-nephew.

**Q4. (a) M × N ÷ T.** M × N = M is mother of N; N ÷ T = N is father of T. So M is the mother of T's father = grandmother of T.
**Trick:** For "grandmother," you need mother-of-(father/mother-of-target). Chain a "mother" symbol into a "parent" symbol.

**Q5. (b) Wife.** "Only son of my wife's father" = wife's brother... wait, wife's father's only son is the wife's brother, unless the wife's father has no son, but here he does. The woman is the mother of that son. The mother of the wife's brother = the wife's mother.
**Corrected answer: (d) Mother-in-law.** The mother of Sohan's wife's brother is Sohan's wife's mother = Sohan's mother-in-law.
**Trick:** "Only son of wife's father" = wife's brother; his mother = wife's mother = mother-in-law.

**Q6. (b) P × R − Q.** P × R = P is brother of R; R − Q = R is wife of Q. That makes P the brother of Q's wife = Q's brother-in-law, not maternal uncle. Test (a): P × R ÷ Q = P brother of R, R mother of Q => P is mother's brother of Q = maternal uncle.
**Corrected answer: (a) P × R ÷ Q.**
**Trick:** Maternal uncle = brother of the mother. Need "brother" symbol chained into "mother" symbol reaching Q.

**Q7. (a) 2.** Members: F (mother of G, female), G (male), H (brother of G, male), J (wife of G, female), I (male), K (sister of I, female). Females = F, J, K = 3.
**Corrected answer: (b) 3.**
**Trick:** List each member's gender explicitly from the given relations before counting; "sister" and "wife" and "mother" fix female, "brother"/"father"/"husband" fix male.

**Q8. (a) Mother.** "His mother is the only daughter of my mother" => the man's mother is the woman's sister (only daughter of the woman's mother). But if the woman's mother has only one daughter and the woman is speaking, the "only daughter" is the woman herself. So the man's mother is the woman => the woman is the man's mother.
**Trick:** When the speaker is female and the clue says "only daughter of my mother," that only daughter is usually the speaker herself.

**Q9. (a) Cousin.** A is son of B. C is B's sister, C's son is D. A and D are children of a brother-sister pair (B and C), so A and D are cousins.
**Trick:** Children of two siblings are cousins to each other.

**Q10. (a) Sister.** "Father of her brother" = the lady's father. "Only son of my grandfather" = Ravi's father. So the lady's father = Ravi's father => the lady is Ravi's sister.
**Trick:** "Father of her brother" is just "her father." Equate the two fathers; shared father => siblings.

### Direction Sense

**Q11. (a) 3 km East.** North 5 => turn right (East) 3 => turn right (South) 5. The two 5 km vertical legs cancel (5 North then 5 South). Net displacement = 3 km East.
**Trick:** Opposite equal legs cancel; only the unmatched East leg survives.

**Q12. (a) 5 km.** East 4, then left = North 3. Right triangle legs 4 and 3 => hypotenuse = √(16+9) = 5.
**Trick:** 3-4-5 Pythagorean triple — memorize it for instant hypotenuse.

**Q13. (c) North, 5 m.** South 12 => left (East) 5 => left (North) 12. The two 12 m legs (South then North) cancel; net = 5 m East of start. He is now facing North. Distance from start = 5 m.
**Note:** Direction facing = North (correct); distance = 5 m. Displacement direction is East, but the option pairs facing-direction with distance.
**Corrected answer: (c) North, 5 m** — he faces North and is 5 m from start.
**Trick:** Track "facing" separately from "displacement." Two equal opposite legs cancel, leaving the single perpendicular leg as the distance.

**Q14. (a) 8 m North.** North 10, East 6, South 2, West 6. East 6 and West 6 cancel. North 10 minus South 2 = 8 North.
**Trick:** Sum vertical legs (10−2=8 N) and horizontal legs (6−6=0) separately.

**Q15. (d) South-West.** A -> B East. B -> C South. C -> D West. D is South of A (from the C South leg) and West of A (D is west of C which is east-aligned with... ) Let A=(0,0), B=(x,0), C=(x,−y), D=(x−z,−y). If z can exceed x, D could be west of A. With unknown magnitudes, D is generally to the South and to the West => South-West. Standard puzzle answer: South-West.
**Trick:** Assign coordinates. Down-and-left of the origin => South-West quadrant.

**Q16. (a) 3 m.** North 9, right (East) 4, right (South) 12, right (West) 4. East 4 and West 4 cancel. North 9 then South 12 => net 3 m South of start. Distance = 3 m.
**Trick:** Vertical: 9 N − 12 S = 3 S. Horizontal legs cancel. Distance = 3 m.

**Q17. (a) 21 m.** Both start at origin and both move 8 m East, so their horizontal positions match — East legs cancel in the comparison. Amit is 15 m North; Sumit is 6 m South. Vertical gap = 15 + 6 = 21 m.
**Trick:** Equal common legs (both 8 E) drop out; add the opposite vertical distances 15 + 6 = 21.

**Q18. (a) 25 km.** East 20 => right (South) 15 => right (West) 20 => left (South) 10. East 20 and West 20 cancel. South 15 + South 10 = 25 South. Distance = 25 km.
**Trick:** After cancelling the equal E/W legs, add the two southward legs: 15+10 = 25.

### Order & Ranking

**Q36. (b) 29th.** Position from right = Total − Position from left + 1 = 40 − 12 + 1 = 29.
**Trick:** Right rank = (Total − Left rank + 1). The "+1" counts the person themselves.

**Q37. (c) 34.** Total = (rank from top) + (rank from bottom) − 1 = 15 + 20 − 1 = 34.
**Trick:** Both ranks count the person once; subtract the double-count: 15+20−1.

**Q38. (c) 23.** Amit was 7th from left; after moving to Bina's spot he is 15th from left, i.e. Bina's position was 15th from left. Bina is 9th from the right. Total = left rank + right rank − 1 = 15 + 9 − 1 = 23.
