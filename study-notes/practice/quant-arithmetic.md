## Answer Key, Solutions & Tricks

**Q1 — (a) Rs 6125.**
SI for (8 − 5) = 3 years = 12005 − 9800 = Rs 2205, so SI per year = 2205 / 3 = Rs 735.
SI for 5 years = 5 × 735 = 3675. Principal = 9800 − 3675 = **Rs 6125**.
*Trick:* **SI for T years = (1-year interest) × T.** Difference of two amounts gives interest for the gap in years directly; peel off SI to reach the principal.

**Q2 — (b) Rs 6000.**
CI − SI for 2 years = P(R/100)^2 = P × (10/100)^2 = P/100. So P/100 = 60 → P = **Rs 6000**.
*Trick:* **CI − SI (2 yrs) = P(R/100)^2.** The extra is just interest-on-interest of one year.

**Q3 — (b) Rs 6360.**
Two-year effective CI rate = 2R + R^2/100 = 24 + 144/100 = 25.44%.
CI = 25000 × 25.44/100 = **Rs 6360**.
*Trick:* **CI for 2 years = (2R + R^2/100)% of P** — no need to compute amounts separately.

**Q4 — (b) 26%.**
SP = CP × (140/100) × (90/100) = CP × 1.26. Profit = **26%**.
*Trick:* **SP = CP × multiplier chain.** Chain markup and discount as multipliers; the base is always CP.

**Q5 — (b) Rs 920.**
CP = 720 / 0.90 = Rs 800. SP for 15% gain = 800 × 1.15 = **Rs 920**.
*Trick:* **Base is CP; SP = CP × multiplier.** Recover CP from the loss SP first, then re-apply the gain multiplier.

**Q6 — (b) 1% loss.**
CP1 = 990/1.1 = 900, CP2 = 990/0.9 = 1100; total CP = 2000, total SP = 1980; loss = 20/2000 = **1%**.
*Trick:* Equal SP with equal +x% and −x% always gives a **net loss of (x^2/100)%**. Here 10^2/100 = 1%.

**Q7 — (a) 7.2 days.**
LCM(12, 18) = 36 units. Rates: A = 3, B = 2, together = 5 units/day. Time = 36/5 = **7.2 days**.
*Trick:* **LCM method** — set total work = LCM of the days, convert each worker to units/day, add rates.

**Q8 — (c) 60 days.**
Total work = LCM(15, 20) = 60. Together = 4, A = 3, so B = 4 − 3 = 1 unit/day. Time = 60/1 = **60 days**.
*Trick:* **B's rate = (combined rate) − (A's rate)** on the LCM units.

**Q9 — (c) 16 men.**
Total work = 12 × 8 = 96 man-days. Men needed for 6 days = 96/6 = **16**.
*Trick:* **Men × Days = constant** (M1·D1 = M2·D2) when the work is fixed.

**Q10 — (b) 20 s.**
Speed = 72 × 5/18 = 20 m/s. Distance to cross = 180 + 220 = 400 m. Time = 400/20 = **20 s**.
*Trick:* **km/hr → m/s: multiply by 5/18.** To cross a platform, distance = train length + platform length.

**Q11 — (b) 7 km/hr.**
Downstream = 24/3 = 8 km/hr, upstream = 24/4 = 6 km/hr. Still-water speed = (8 + 6)/2 = **7 km/hr**.
*Trick:* **Boat speed = (down + up)/2**, stream speed = (down − up)/2.

**Q12 — (b) 48 km/hr.**
Average speed = 2xy/(x+y) = 2 × 40 × 60 / (40 + 60) = 4800/100 = **48 km/hr**.
*Trick:* **Equal distance both ways → average speed = 2xy/(x+y)** (harmonic mean), never the plain average.

**Q13 — (b) 35.**
Sum of 5 = 5 × 27 = 135; sum of 4 = 4 × 25 = 100. Excluded = 135 − 100 = **35**.
*Trick:* **Sum = average × count.** Work with totals, then subtract.

**Q14 — (b) 52 years.**
New average = 30 for 12 people → total = 360. Old total = 11 × 28 = 308. Coach = 360 − 308 = **52**.
*Trick shortcut:* new member's value = **new average + (increase × old count)** = 30 + 2 × 11 = 52.

**Q15 — (b) 73.5.**
Average of first n multiples of k = k(n+1)/2 = 7 × 21/2 = **73.5**.
*Trick:* **Average of first n multiples of k = k(n+1)/2**; for an evenly spaced list, average = (first + last)/2 = (7 + 140)/2.

**Q16 — (b) 30 years.**
Let ages be 5x and 7x. (5x + 6)/(7x + 6) = 3/4 → 4(5x + 6) = 3(7x + 6) → 20x + 24 = 21x + 18 → x = 6. A = 5 × 6 = **30**.
*Trick:* Convert the ratio to 5x, 7x, add the years, cross-multiply the new ratio, solve for x.

**Q17 — (b) 15 years.**
Let son = S, father = 3S. After 5 yrs: 3S + 5 = 2.5(S + 5) → 3S + 5 = 2.5S + 12.5 → 0.5S = 7.5 → S = **15**.
*Trick:* Express both current ages in one variable, add the same years to each, then equate to the new multiple.

**Q18 — (b) Rs 600.**
Total parts = 2 + 3 + 5 = 10. C's share = (5/10) × 1200 = **Rs 600**.
*Trick:* **Each share = (its ratio part / total parts) × total amount.**

**Q19 — (a) 9 : 12 : 10.**
Make b common: a:b = 3:4 = 9:12, b:c = 6:5 = 12:10. So a:b:c = **9 : 12 : 10**.
*Trick:* **Scale each ratio so the linking term (b) matches** (LCM of 4 and 6 = 12), then read straight across.

**Q20 — (b) 4500.**
Winner 60%, loser 40%; margin = 20% of total = 900 → total = 900 × 100/20 = **4500**.
*Trick:* **Winning margin % = (win% − lose%)**; total = margin votes × 100 / margin%.

**Q21 — (b) 20%.**
Required reduction = increase/(100 + increase) × 100 = 25/125 × 100 = **20%**.
*Trick:* To undo an x% rise, reduce by **x/(100 + x) × 100%**. (For 25% it is the classic 20%.)

**Q22 — (b) 2 : 1.**
By alligation, ratio = (45 − 35) : (35 − 30) = 10 : 5 = **2 : 1** (cheaper : dearer).
*Trick:* **Alligation cross** — cheaper : dearer = (dearer − mean) : (mean − cheaper).

**Q23 — (b) 32.4 litres.**
Milk left = 40 × (1 − 4/40)^2 = 40 × (0.9)^2 = 40 × 0.81 = **32.4 litres**.
*Trick:* **Replacement (repeated) formula: final = initial × (1 − removed/total)^n.**

**Q24 — (b) 4 : 3.**
Capital × time: A = 20000 × 12 = 240000; B = 30000 × 6 = 180000. Ratio = 240 : 180 = **4 : 3**.
*Trick:* **Profit share ∝ capital × months invested.** Weight each partner by money-time, then reduce.

**Q25 — (b) Rs 3000.**
Equal time, so ratio = 6000 : 8000 : 10000 = 3 : 4 : 5 (total 12). C = (5/12) × 7200 = **Rs 3000**.
*Trick:* Same duration → **profit ratio = investment ratio**; each share = (part/total) × profit.
