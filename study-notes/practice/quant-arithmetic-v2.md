## Answer Key, Solutions & Tricks

### Simple & Compound Interest

**1. (b) Rs 3,000.**
SI = (P x R x T)/100 = (12500 x 8 x 3)/100 = 300000/100 = **3,000**.
**Formula/Trick:** SI = PRT/100. Just multiply P by R by T and drop two zeros.

**2. (b) Rs 3,150.**
Amount = P(1 + R/100)^T = 15000 x (1.1)^2 = 15000 x 1.21 = 18,150. CI = 18150 − 15000 = **3,150**.
**Formula/Trick:** For 2 years, CI% = 2R + R²/100 = 20 + 1 = 21% of P = 0.21 x 15000 = 3,150.

**3. (c) Rs 20,000.**
Difference for 2 years = P(R/100)² = P x (12/100)² = P x 0.0144 = 288, so P = 288/0.0144 = **20,000**.
**Formula/Trick:** CI − SI (2 yrs) = P(R/100)². Here P = 288 x 10000/144 = 20,000.

**4. (c) 12.5%.**
To double at SI, interest = principal, so PRT/100 = P → R x 8 = 100 → R = 100/8 = **12.5%**.
**Formula/Trick:** Rate to multiply to n times in T years at SI = (n−1) x 100 / T = (2−1) x 100 / 8 = 12.5%.

**5. (b) Rs 8,000.**
P(1.1)² = 9680 → P x 1.21 = 9680 → P = 9680/1.21 = **8,000**.
**Formula/Trick:** P = Amount / (1 + R/100)^T. Divide 9680 by 1.21.

### Profit & Loss

**6. (c) 15%.**
Profit = 920 − 800 = 120. Profit% = 120/800 x 100 = **15%**.
**Formula/Trick:** Profit% = (SP − CP)/CP x 100.

**7. (c) Rs 1,200.**
CP = SP/(1 − loss%/100) = 1140/0.95 = **1,200**.
**Formula/Trick:** At a loss, CP = SP x 100/(100 − loss%) = 1140 x 100/95 = 1,200.

**8. (b) 5% profit.**
Take CP = 100. MP = 140. SP = 140 x 0.75 = 105. Profit = 105 − 100 = **5% profit**.
**Formula/Trick:** Net factor = (1 + m/100)(1 − d/100) = 1.4 x 0.75 = 1.05 → 5% gain.

**9. (b) 1% loss.**
Selling two items at equal price with equal +x% and −x% always gives a loss of x²/100 = 10²/100 = **1% loss**.
(Check: CP₁ = 990/1.1 = 900, CP₂ = 990/0.9 = 1100; total CP = 2000, total SP = 1980 → 20 loss on 2000 = 1%.)
**Formula/Trick:** Same SP, gain x% and loss x% → always x²/100 loss.

**10. (c) 25%.**
CP of 15 = SP of 12. Profit% = (15 − 12)/12 x 100 = 3/12 x 100 = **25%**.
**Formula/Trick:** If CP of A articles = SP of B articles, Profit% = (A − B)/B x 100.

### Time & Work

**11. (b) 7.2 days.**
Combined rate = 1/12 + 1/18 = 3/36 + 2/36 = 5/36. Time = 36/5 = **7.2 days**.
**Formula/Trick:** Together time = (ab)/(a + b) = (12 x 18)/30 = 216/30 = 7.2.

**12. (c) 30 days.**
B's rate = 1/10 − 1/15 = 3/30 − 2/30 = 1/30 → **30 days**.
**Formula/Trick:** B = (A x AB)/(A − AB) = (15 x 10)/(15 − 10) = 150/5 = 30.

**13. (c) 21 days.**
A:B efficiency = 2:1, combined 3 units/day. Total work = 3 x 14 = 42 units. A alone = 42/2 = **21 days**.
**Formula/Trick:** More efficient one alone = together time x (sum of ratio)/(its own ratio) = 14 x 3/2 = 21.

**14. (c) 12 days.**
Man-days = 15 x 20 = 300. With 25 men: 300/25 = **12 days**.
**Formula/Trick:** M₁D₁ = M₂D₂ → D₂ = (15 x 20)/25 = 12.

**15. (c) 16 days.**
A does 6/24 = 1/4 in 6 days. Remaining 3/4 done by B in 12 days → B's full-work time = 12 ÷ (3/4) = **16 days**.
**Formula/Trick:** If a fraction f is done in t days, whole job = t/f = 12/(3/4) = 16.

### Speed, Distance, Trains & Boats

**16. (c) 54 km/h.**
Speed = 180/12 = 15 m/s = 15 x 18/5 = **54 km/h**.
**Formula/Trick:** m/s to km/h: multiply by 18/5.

**17. (c) 150 m.**
72 km/h = 72 x 5/18 = 20 m/s. Distance in 20 s = 400 m. Train = 400 − 250 = **150 m**.
**Formula/Trick:** Train length = (speed in m/s x time) − platform length.

**18. (c) 12 s.**
Opposite direction → relative speed = 40 + 50 = 90 km/h = 25 m/s. Total length = 300 m. Time = 300/25 = **12 s**.
**Formula/Trick:** Crossing time = (L₁ + L₂)/relative speed; add speeds when opposite.

**19. (c) 12.5 km/h.**
Downstream = 30/2 = 15 km/h; upstream = 30/3 = 10 km/h. Still water = (15 + 10)/2 = **12.5 km/h**.
**Formula/Trick:** Boat speed = (down + up)/2.

**20. (c) 5 hours.**
Down = 15 + 3 = 18 km/h → 36/18 = 2 h. Up = 15 − 3 = 12 km/h → 36/12 = 3 h. Total = **5 hours**.
**Formula/Trick:** Downstream = b + s, upstream = b − s; add the two times.

### Averages

**21. (c) 28.**
For 5 consecutive even numbers the middle one is the average = 24, so numbers are 20, 22, 24, 26, 28 → largest = **28**.
**Formula/Trick:** For an odd count of equally-spaced numbers, average = middle term; largest = average + 2 x (gap x steps) = 24 + 4 = 28.

**22. (c) 45 years.**
Students' total = 30 x 14 = 420. New total = 31 x 15 = 465. Teacher = 465 − 420 = **45**.
**Formula/Trick:** New member's value = new avg + (old count) x (rise) = 15 + 30 x 1 = 45.

**23. (c) 53.**
Adding the same 8 to every number raises the average by 8: 45 + 8 = **53**.
**Formula/Trick:** Add k to each item → average increases by k.

**24. (c) 38.**
Total after 17 innings = 16 x 36 + 70 = 576 + 70 = 646. New average = 646/17 = **38**.
**Formula/Trick:** New avg = (old total + new score)/new count.

### Ages

**25. (c) 30 years.**
Let ages be 5x and 7x. (5x + 6)/(7x + 6) = 3/4 → 20x + 24 = 21x + 18 → x = 6. A = 5 x 6 = **30**.
**Formula/Trick:** Cross-multiply the future ratio and solve for x.

**26. (c) 5 years.**
Let son = s, father = 4s. 4s + 10 = 2(s + 10) → 4s + 10 = 2s + 20 → 2s = 10 → s = **5**.
(Check: 20 & 5 now; 30 & 15 later → twice.)
**Formula/Trick:** Frame one equation from the "after 10 years" condition.

**27. (c) 13 years.**
M + D = 50 and M − 5 = 4(D − 5) → M = 4D − 15. Then 4D − 15 + D = 50 → 5D = 65 → D = **13**.
(Check: M = 37, D = 13; 5 yrs ago 32 = 4 x 8.)
**Formula/Trick:** Substitute the sum relation into the past-age relation.

**28. (c) 30 years.**
A = 3B. (3B + 5) + (B + 5) = 50 → 4B + 10 = 50 → B = 10, A = **30**.
**Formula/Trick:** Write both future ages, add, and solve.

### Ratio & Proportion

**29. (c) Rs 1,600.**
Total parts = 2 + 3 + 4 = 9. C = 4/9 x 3600 = **1,600**.
**Formula/Trick:** Share = (own ratio/total ratio) x amount.

**30. (b) 24 and 40.**
Let 3x, 5x. (3x + 8)/(5x + 8) = 2/3 → 9x + 24 = 10x + 16 → x = 8. Numbers = 24 and 40.
**Formula/Trick:** Cross-multiply after adding the constant to each term.

**31. (c) 8:12:15.**
A:B = 2:3, B:C = 4:5. Make B common (LCM 12): A:B = 8:12, B:C = 12:15 → A:B:C = **8:12:15**.
**Formula/Trick:** Scale each ratio so the shared term (B) matches.

**32. (c) 72.**
Boys − girls = 7x − 5x = 2x = 12 → x = 6. Total = 12x = **72**.
**Formula/Trick:** Difference in parts equals given difference → find x, multiply by total parts.

### Percentages

**33. (c) 400.**
Number = 140/0.35 = 140 x 100/35 = **400**.
**Formula/Trick:** If p% of N = value, then N = value x 100/p.

**34. (c) 4,000.**
Winner 60%, loser 40%, margin = 20% = 800 votes → total = 800 x 100/20 = **4,000**.
**Formula/Trick:** Margin% = winner% − loser%; total = margin votes x 100/margin%.

**35. (c) 16⅔%.**
Required reduction = increase/(100 + increase) x 100 = 20/120 x 100 = **16⅔%**.
**Formula/Trick:** To undo an x% rise, reduce by [x/(100 + x)] x 100.

**36. (c) 400.**
Pass mark = 40%, student got 30% and fell short by 40 marks → 10% of max = 40 → max = **400**.
**Formula/Trick:** (pass% − scored%) of max = shortfall; max = shortfall x 100/(difference%).

**37. (c) 1% decrease.**
Net factor = 1.10 x 0.90 = 0.99 → **1% decrease**.
**Formula/Trick:** Successive +a% and −b%: net = a − b − (ab/100) = 10 − 10 − 1 = −1%.

### Mixtures & Alligation

**38. (c) 1:2.**
Alligation: cheaper 30, dearer 45, mean 40. Ratio (cheap:dear) = (45 − 40):(40 − 30) = 5:10 = **1:2**.
**Formula/Trick:** Ratio = (dearer − mean):(mean − cheaper).

**39. (c) 10 litres.**
In 60 L: milk = 42, water = 18. Milk unchanged; for 3:2 water must be 42 x 2/3 = 28. Add 28 − 18 = **10 litres**.
**Formula/Trick:** Keep the unchanged quantity fixed, compute the new required amount of the other.

**40. (c) 25.6 litres.**
Milk left = 40 x (1 − 8/40)² = 40 x (0.8)² = 40 x 0.64 = **25.6 litres**.
**Formula/Trick:** After n replacements: remaining = Initial x (1 − drawn/total)^n.

**41. (c) 1:4.**
To gain 25% selling at Rs 24, cost of mixture = 24/1.25 = Rs 19.2/L. Alligation of water (0) and milk (24) about 19.2: water:milk = (24 − 19.2):(19.2 − 0) = 4.8:19.2 = **1:4**.
**Formula/Trick:** Cost of mix = SP/(1 + gain%); then alligate water (cost 0) with milk.

### Pipes & Cisterns

**46. (c) 12 minutes.**
Combined rate = 1/20 + 1/30 = 3/60 + 2/60 = 5/60 = 1/12 → **12 minutes**.
**Formula/Trick:** Together time = (ab)/(a + b) = (20 x 30)/50 = 12.

**47. (c) 60 hours.**
Net rate = 1/15 − 1/20 = 4/60 − 3/60 = 1/60 → **60 hours**.
**Formula/Trick:** Fill minus leak: net = 1/fill − 1/leak; time = reciprocal.

**48. (c) 8 minutes.**
A runs the full 18 min → 18/24 = 3/4 filled. B must fill remaining 1/4 → B's time t/32 = 1/4 → t = **8 minutes**.
**Formula/Trick:** Fraction by the always-open pipe first, then find how long the other pipe supplies the rest.

**49. (c) 40 hours.**
With leak it takes 8 + 2 = 10 h. Leak rate = 1/8 − 1/10 = 5/40 − 4/40 = 1/40 → **40 hours**.
**Formula/Trick:** Leak rate = 1/(fill time) − 1/(actual time); invert for emptying time.

**50. (c) 5 minutes.**
Combined = 1/10 + 1/15 + 1/30 = 3/30 + 2/30 + 1/30 = 6/30 = 1/5 → **5 minutes**.
**Formula/Trick:** Add the individual rates (use LCM as the work unit) and take the reciprocal.
