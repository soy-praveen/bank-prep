## Answer Key, Solutions & Tricks

1. **(b) 65.** BODMAS: multiply/divide first — 24×3 = 72, 48÷6 = 8. Then 72 + 8 − 15 = 65.
   **Trick:** Resolve all × and ÷ into single numbers before touching + and −; then it is plain addition/subtraction.

2. **(c) 51.** √576 = 24, √729 = 27. Sum = 51.
   **Trick:** Memorise perfect-square roots to 30² and cube roots to 20³. 24² = 576, 27² = 729 are staples.

3. **(b) 120.** a² − b² = (a+b)(a−b) = (17+13)(17−13) = 30 × 4 = 120.
   **Trick:** Never square each term; the difference-of-squares factorisation is far faster.

4. **(a) 216.** 12³/8 = (12/2)³ = 6³ = 216.
   **Trick:** Since 8 = 2³, dividing a cube by 2³ just halves the base before cubing.

5. **(c) 192.** 448/7 = 64, then ×3 = 192.
   **Trick:** Divide by the denominator first (448÷7 is clean), then multiply — smaller intermediate numbers.

6. **(b) 224.** 35% = 0.35; 640 × 0.35 = 224. Or 10% = 64, 30% = 192, 5% = 32, sum 224.
   **Trick:** Build percentages from 10% and 5% chunks to avoid decimal multiplication.

7. **(c) 84.** Brackets first: 18+6 = 24; 24×4 = 96; 60÷5 = 12; 96 − 12 = 84.
   **Trick:** Clear the innermost bracket, then × and ÷, then −.

8. **(b) 28.** ∛4096 = 16 (16³ = 4096), ∛1728 = 12. Sum = 28.
   **Trick:** 16³ = 4096 and 12³ = 1728 recur constantly — keep them at hand.

9. **(c) 90.** (2/5)(3/4) = 6/20 = 3/10; 3/10 × 300 = 90.
   **Trick:** Cancel across the chain first (300/20 = 15, ×6 = 90) — no big multiplications.

10. **(b) 90.** 40% of 900 = 360; 25% of 360 = 90.
    **Trick:** Order does not matter for chained percentages; take the "friendliest" first (25% = ÷4).

11. **(b) 263.** √1444 = 38 (38² = 1444), 15² = 225. Sum = 263.
    **Trick:** 38² = 1444 — for roots ending in 4, the root ends in 2 or 8; 1444 is just below 40²=1600, so 38.

12. **(b) 23.** 144÷12 = 12, 15×4 = 60, 7² = 49. So 12 + 60 − 49 = 23.
    **Trick:** Evaluate each ×/÷/power block independently, then combine signs at the end.

13. **(b) 410.** (5/8) of 512 = 5 × 64 = 320; (3/7) of 210 = 3 × 30 = 90. Sum = 410.
    **Trick:** Pick numerators that leave clean divisions (512÷8, 210÷7) — mental one-liners.

14. **(b) 106.** 12.5% = 1/8; 848/8 = 106.
    **Trick:** Convert "ugly" percentages to unit fractions: 12.5% = 1/8, 6.25% = 1/16, 6.66% = 1/15.

15. **(b) 452.** 14² = 196, 16² = 256. Sum = 452.
    **Trick:** For two squares straddling 15, note 14²+16² = 2(15²)+2 = 450+2 = 452 (a²+b² with mean 15, gap 1).

16. **(b) 176.** √256 = 16, √121 = 11; 16 × 11 = 176.
    **Trick:** √(256×121) = √30976 is the same; but rooting each factor separately is instant.

17. **(b) 39.** Left to right for equal-priority ×/÷: 63÷9 = 7, ×4 = 28, +11 = 39.
    **Trick:** ÷ and × share priority — process strictly left to right, do not multiply before dividing.

18. **(b) 112.** 8³ = 512, 20² = 400. 512 − 400 = 112.
    **Trick:** Keep small cubes (8³=512, 7³=343, 9³=729) memorised for these mixed power problems.

19. **(b) 330.** (7/9) of 630 = 7 × 70 = 490; (2/3) of 240 = 160. 490 − 160 = 330.
    **Trick:** 630÷9 = 70 and 240÷3 = 80 are clean — always divide before multiplying the numerator.

20. **(b) 190.** 40% of 250 = 100; 60% of 150 = 90. Sum = 190.
    **Trick:** 40% of 250 = 40% × 250 = 25% × 400 style swaps ("x% of y = y% of x") can simplify — here 250×0.4 = 100 directly.

21. **(c) 195.** Round: 30% of 650 = 195. (Exact ≈ 194.1.)
    **Trick:** Round the percent to a clean value and the base to a round number, then multiply.

22. **(b) 700.** 4899 ≈ 4900, 6.98 ≈ 7; 4900/7 = 700.
    **Trick:** In division, nudge both numbers so they cancel evenly (7 divides 4900).

23. **(c) 64.** 4095 ≈ 4096 = 64². So √4095 ≈ 64.
    **Trick:** Spot the nearest perfect square; 4096 = 64² is a classic anchor.

24. **(b) 360.** 15 × 24 = 360. (Exact ≈ 360.1.)
    **Trick:** Round each factor to the nearest integer; the tiny +0.02/−0.02 offsets nearly cancel.

25. **(b) 512.** 64% of 800 = 512.
    **Trick:** 64% of 800 = 8 × 64 = 512 (since 1% of 800 = 8).

26. **(c) 80.** 1200/15 = 80.
    **Trick:** Round the dividend up to a multiple of the rounded divisor (1200 is 15×80).

27. **(b) 1225.** 35² = 1225.
    **Trick:** Squares ending in 5: n5² = (n)(n+1) then append 25 → 3×4 = 12, so 1225.

28. **(c) 255.** 17% of 1500 = 255.
    **Trick:** 1% of 1500 = 15, so 17% = 15 × 17 = 255.

29. **(c) 243.** 45% of 320 = 144; 11 × 9 = 99. Sum = 144 + 99 = 243.
    **Trick:** Handle each bracket with its own rounding, then add the rounded results.

30. **(c) 100.** 8888 ≈ 8900, 88.9 ≈ 89; 8900/89 = 100.
    **Trick:** Recognise 89 × 100 = 8900 — round the dividend to hit an exact multiple.

31. **(c) 12.** 1727 ≈ 1728 = 12³. So ∛1727 ≈ 12.
    **Trick:** 12³ = 1728 is the nearest cube anchor.

32. **(c) 450.** 25 × 16 = 400; + 50 = 450.
    **Trick:** 25 × 16 = 25 × 16 = 400 (25 × 4 = 100, ×4 = 400); add the rounded 49.9 ≈ 50.

33. **(c) 200.** 80% of 250 = 200.
    **Trick:** 80% = 4/5; (4/5) × 250 = 200.

34. **(c) 25.** 624.8 ≈ 625 = 25². So √624.8 ≈ 25.
    **Trick:** 625 = 25² — one of the most common approximation anchors.

35. **(c) 600.** 3600/6 = 600.
    **Trick:** Round dividend to 3600 (a multiple of 6) and divisor to 6.

36. **(b) 150.** 12 × 15 = 180; − 30 = 150.
    **Trick:** Multiply the rounded factors, then apply the rounded subtraction last.

37. **(c) 242.** 55% of 440 = 242.
    **Trick:** 55% = 11/20; (11/20) × 440 = 11 × 22 = 242.

38. **(b) 130.** 7² + 9² = 49 + 81 = 130.
    **Trick:** Round each base to the nearest integer before squaring — 6.98→7, 9.02→9.

39. **(c) 130.** 720/8 = 90; + 40 = 130.
    **Trick:** Do the division block first (720÷8 = 90), then add the rounded 39.9 ≈ 40.

40. **(c) 216.** 6³ = 216.
    **Trick:** Three near-equal factors ≈ 6 each → cube it: 6³ = 216.
