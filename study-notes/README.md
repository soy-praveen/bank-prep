## The exam (verified pattern)

| Section | Q | Marks | Time | Your target |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| English | 40 | 40 | 20 min | 30–34 |
| Quant | 30 | 30 | 20 min | 20–25 |
| Reasoning | 30 | 30 | 20 min | 22–26 |
| **Total** | 100 | 100 | 60 min | **~75+** |

- Sectional timers (20 min locked per section). **Negative marking: −0.25 per wrong.**
- Prelims is **qualifying** — clearing cutoff is typically well below 75. 75 is the "comfortably ahead" target.
- Order of attempt on exam day: **English → Reasoning → Quant.**

## Study principle (drilled hard)

**Speed is not your problem — accuracy is.** You're consistently faster than exam pace. The marks leak from careless slips and reference-flips, not from being slow. **Slow down ~20%, verify, and you have a huge time buffer to do it.**
