## LEAKS — your paper-practice homework (do these SOLO)

These are things where the *method* is understood but *fluency* needs volume. Grind from the 11 PYQ papers in the app.

### Leak 3 — DI accuracy
- "% of" (A/B) vs "% more/less than" ((diff)/base) — don't confuse them.
- Read the LAST word (total vs average vs ratio); answer in the exact form asked.
- Trace exact row AND column before pulling a cell.

### Leak 4 — SI & CI (target ~100 sums)
SI basic + doubling/tripling + reverse (find P/R/T); CI 2–3yr + CI−SI difference + reverse-for-principal.

### Leak 5 — Arithmetic toolkit
P&L, Averages, Time-Work, Speed-Distance, Ages, Mixtures — volume practice with formulas.

## Exam-day strategy

- **Order: English → Reasoning → Quant.** Bank your strength calm, then hunt.
- Per section: secure the fast/standalone stuff first, attempt only the easiest 1–2 puzzles/DI sets, **skip monsters** (a set not cracking in ~1 min = leave it).
- Negative marking 0.25: a rushed wrong answer swings 1.25 marks. **Accuracy > attempts.**
- You have a big time buffer — use it to verify, not to attempt every hard question.
